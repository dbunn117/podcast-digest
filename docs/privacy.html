Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

What this covers

Scout Hermes ("the app") is a personal AI assistant that operates on David Bunn's private infrastructure. It accesses Google services (Gmail, Calendar, Drive) solely on behalf of David Bunn to manage his personal email, calendar events, and documents.

Data access and use

The app accesses the following Google data:

  • Gmail — to read, triage, and draft emails for David Bunn's personal account.
  • Google Calendar — to read and create events for scheduling purposes.
  • Google Drive — to read and write documents and files.

All data access is limited to David Bunn's own Google account. No third parties have access to this data. The app does not share, sell, or transmit any data to external services beyond the Google APIs it is authorized to use, and the AI model providers (OpenRouter, Anthropic) that process natural-language instructions — both of which are subject to their own data-handling policies and are used exclusively for David's personal assistant functions.

Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the server where the app runs. No user data is stored in a database or transmitted to any analytics service.
